THE GAP

Knowing a tool is not the same as knowing how to use it.

The digital economy creates opportunities, but getting ready for those opportunities takes more than watching tutorials or collecting certificates.

People need room to practise, build, receive feedback, learn with others and understand how their skills work in real situations. That is the gap TELICA is designed to address.

Learn. Practice. Build.
Then keep growing.

A practical learning cycle designed to move people beyond passive learning and toward useful competence.
